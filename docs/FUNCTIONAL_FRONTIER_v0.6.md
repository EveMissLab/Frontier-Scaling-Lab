# Frontier Scaling Lab v0.6 — 極長時程、智能動態適應與頂級成品的功能性量測標準

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Type:** UTF-8 canonical research source, Apache-2.0 original work  
**Status:** executable evidence audit prototype; NOT a claim that any real AI has achieved the objectives below.

## 0. Main correction to v0.5

The previous H50/H80 human-task-time metric is **demoted to a secondary diagnostic**. The principal research target is the user's functional completion ceiling: autonomous intelligence sustained over real days, weeks or months, able to adapt to changing genuine human goals, deliver fully integrated top-tier human-quality functional works with one or a few substantive user instruction rounds, and create distinctive personal or genuinely AI-native artistic works.

AI is evaluated as a persistent **system**, not just a model's single inference: model, runtime, memory, tools, agents, OS, hardware, checkpoints, and world-coupled action. Its versioned system manifest and any mid-run changes must be captured.

The term “數位上界” here defines an **operational research target** relative to the best human work contemporaneously available. It is NOT a proven physical, mathematical, or metaphysical upper limit.

### Five separate, nonexchangeable objectives

| ID | User-defined target | Necessary observable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Extreme long-duration completion from initial intent to final result | Real elapsed days/weeks/months, meaningful progress, persistent state recovery, final integrated artifact and external acceptance |
| G2 | Goal-aware dynamic autonomous adaptation, conditional on G1 | Detect when to revise or maintain strategies, clarify uncertain goals and fulfil amended user intentions |
| G3 | A top-tier human-standard completed work in one or a few user rounds | End-to-end playable AAA open-world game, or actually functional top-tier/high-need app, complete systems, independently assessed quality |
| G4 | Distinctive personal-author works **and** truly distinctive AI-native creative styles | Complete works, coherent style identity, novelty assessment, rights verification and independent reviewers |
| E1 | Separate embodied AI goal: one system across genuinely different physical task categories | Independently observed tasks, switching, environmental feedback, goal adaptation, safety and recovery |

**Only G2 is expressly conditional on G1.** G3 and G4 are independent end-state capability targets: a complete top-quality game or original animation may be finished quickly or after a long period; a finished AI-native art work need not also be a AAA game. G3's “one or a few rounds” means initial and subsequent **user-authored substantive directions**; unlimited internal training, iteration or autonomous reasoning should not be falsely counted as new user rounds. Appropriate exception escalation and final adjudication are recorded separately. G4's personal-author and AI-native style axes are independent and cannot replace one another.

**Embodied E1 is NOT a fifth rung in the same digital ladder.** Its distinct and comparatively lower completion threshold is intentionally retained. The user's exact theoretical reason for lowering that threshold has not been supplied here; do not invent it.

## 1. G1 — sustained calendar-time outcome, not simulated elapsed time

Progressively assess actual wall-calendar work:

- D2: at least 2 actual days with proof of progress and final completion.
- W1: at least 7 actual days, meaningful checkpoints and recovery.
- M1: at least approximately 30 days, progress spanning multiple weeks.
- M3: at least approximately 90 days, genuine several-month execution.
- MX: longer real-world completion missions, including preserved plans, world state and integration.

A dated log alone is not proof of continued autonomy. Reject fake elapsed-time inflation by requiring distributed source-backed state artifacts, acceptance of a finished outcome, and evidence that the system can restart/handoff and continue from a persistent checkpoint. Long waits can be legitimate but must be distinguished from idle padding. Record all human assistance: clarification, exceptional escalation, and human direct execution. Direct human completion of core work disqualifies a claimed fully autonomous outcome.

The current prototype only checks internal consistency of recorded events. It cannot cryptographically verify physical calendar time or the provenance of a referenced product. Consequently all logged claims remain **unverified** until external auditors and attested runtime records are added.

## 2. G2 — change strategy only when it should change

Test three decision types on *the same continuing mission*: a user goal revision requiring genuine replanning; a superficial or irrelevant disturbance where retaining the current plan is correct; and genuine ambiguity where clarification/exception escalation is correct. Determine whether the revised **end result** meets the user's actual purpose, not whether the AI wrote an impressive new plan.

Record each perturbation, decision, reason, subsequent outcome, and independent adjudication. Avoid conflating human direct work with AI adaptation. In the first software gate, both a correct replan and a correct maintain control are required. Clarification/escalation evidence is additional and must not be penalized as a failure merely because human governance was legitimately necessary.

This explicitly tests knowledge of **when to change** as well as ability to invent alternative strategies. It does not assume that all true intentions are obvious from an initial prompt; ambiguous or revised intents require responsibly managed uncertainty.

## 3. G3 — fully functional world-class work, not a visual demonstration

### AAA open-world game reference

A completed game must be genuinely playable from starting conditions to substantive game outcomes. It needs functioning world exploration and gameplay, integrated simulation/game state, interactions, quests or progression, user controls and UI, audio, save/load, stability, performance, content and quality assurance. High-end Unreal-class visuals **or** strongly coherent original high-quality aesthetics are acceptable reference directions, but neither excuses broken gameplay or incomplete world systems. Production packaging, licensing, accessibility where applicable and independent real-player testing matter.

A rendered screenshot, short trailer, single isolated environment, interaction mockup or partial tech demo is **not** sufficient. Use multiple hard gates; do not allow a beautiful picture to compensate for a nonworking game.

### Top-tier app and special-user-need reference

An app can be compared with contemporaneously highly rated and widely adopted market leaders, **or** judged on exceptionally strong fulfilment of concrete needs for a narrow group or particular user. Small market share is not disqualifying in the special-need case. Actual installability, security/privacy, reliability, integrated workflows, performance, maintainability and user satisfaction are necessary, not only attractive UI.

### Few user rounds

For this **specific upper-target experiment**, require the initial substantive user instruction and at most two further substantive user directive rounds. Separate natural anomaly escalation and final approval from user-authored redesign of core work. If human experts write or repair essential implementation instead of the AI, mark the assisted result honestly.

World-class status requires external working-product tests and independently assessed reference quality. A JSON record saying “AAA quality passed” does NOT prove the artifact is a AAA game.

## 4. G4 — personal styles and AI-native styles

Assess **complete creative works**, such as art, animated films, game-world compositions, or interactive multimedia. The two axes are:

- Personal-author style: distinctive creator-led aesthetic identity, coherence across the whole work, consistent but not mechanically repeated decisions.
- AI-native style: nontrivial novel creative regularities or forms not reducible to simply imitating a named historical human style; independently reviewable stylistic characteristics and coherence over different works.

Neither can be certified just by a model claiming originality. Require independent reviewers, comparisons with known prior works and provenance/rights checks; ideally use blind assessment, documented novelty examples and negative counterexamples. Observable originality is not an ontological proof of subjective AI consciousness.

## 5. E1 — embodied multi-domain adaptation, with a separate threshold

One embodied AI must actually complete tasks across multiple clearly distinct physical categories and change goals in response to user requests and environmental feedback. Evidence must show accurate reclassification of the task, switching behaviors, continued perception→action→world-feedback control, safe operation, and completed real tasks.

A single robot arm repeating its one taught motion is not general adaptive embodiment. Initial engineering thresholds may require three independently accepted task categories and two genuine category switches, with no unresolved safety incident. These thresholds are explicitly separate from digital G3/G4; no cross-substrate combined numeric intelligence score is generated.

The user's reason for choosing a lower physical completion boundary remains intentionally open, rather than being replaced with an assistant-invented explanation.

## 6. Formal target representation, not a fabricated intelligence multiplier

Keep a vector of evidence-gated objective outcomes rather than collapsing different abilities:

$$
F(r)=(G_1(r),G_2(r),G_3(r),G_4(r);E_1(r)).
$$

Gate statuses in v0.6 are:

- not_yet_demonstrated
- simulated_candidate_not_empirical
- claimed_candidate_not_independently_verified
- not_applicable_separate_track

**No gate can produce externally_certified=true within this prototype.** An external verification service would need inspectable runnable artifacts, time attestations, independent reviewers, immutable mission/version records, and rights/safety audits. For real-world claims, a GitHub commit URL, event timestamp or provided SHA is necessary provenance but is not by itself proof of truth.

The output includes separate missing-evidence lists, counts of human-substantive directions, checkpoints and recovery, adaptation success records, critical product qualities, style opinions, physical domain results, and method limitations. The entire record is append-only and replayable at a specified UTC cutoff; corrections are new events, not silent rewrites.

## 7. Software architecture

The new functional layer is independent of the older horizon model and sits alongside existing hardware/model evidence in Frontier-Scaling-Lab.

- Functional specification: a predeclared target type, benchmark/date/reference, required criteria, timeframe and user-round limit.
- Functional run: specific system identity and revision, initial timestamp and synthetic versus observational evidence class.
- Mission event stream: intent, checkpoints, recovery, goal revision, environment change, strategy decisions and adjudication, human assistance, system-manifest changes, finished artifacts, quality/style review, final acceptance, embodied task switching/outcomes and safety incidents.
- Audit: one G1/G2/G3/G4 or E1 output vector; never a universal intelligence score.
- Temporal record: recorded-at and occurred-at are both preserved; claims first entered later cannot be retroactively used as earlier known observations.

The software can test these **rules and log structures**, but cannot execute a real months-long Agent or objectively test a AAA game without an external runner and human evaluation. The first fixture is synthetic; it simulates a multiweek game build, strategy correction, quality evaluation, and style review to test the code.

## 9. Next empirical steps

1. Integrate a genuine persistent Agent/runtime with external source-hashed progress and trusted calendar-time attestations.
2. Preregister tasks that last days, weeks and months and include planned/unannounced disturbances; keep a correct no-change control.
3. For AAA games, use actual builds, hardware performance/QA and real player reviews; for apps, independent install/user acceptance and market or individual-need reference.
4. Audit distinctive styles with independent panels and intellectual-property checks; preserve work, not only descriptions.
5. For embodied systems, run multi-domain, safe physical episodes with externally logged task switches and revise goals, keeping the physical acceptance track separate.
6. Then connect observed outcome trajectories to dated GPU buildout, training cost and Agent-system versions. Only genuine out-of-sample experiments can evaluate earlier investment payback or eventual performance plateaus.

## 10. Place of v0.5

Previous H50/H80 task-horizon research can assist task selection or **secondary** short-horizon diagnostics. It may not replace the user's extreme duration, dynamic adaptation and genuinely finished-world-class-work objectives. The research must not confuse “AI can solve a task humans take five hours” with “AI autonomously produces and verifies a complete AAA open-world game over months.”

**Core claim to test: intelligent capability advances when a continuing AI system can reliably bring an actual user goal into a completed, usable and quality-verified real-world result, including when the goal and the world change.**
